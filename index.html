08 · FAQ

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Message me and I'll answer within a day.

How do I send my footage?

Upload it to Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer and share the link. For big shoots I can send you a folder to upload into.

How many revisions do I get?

Two rounds are included in every package. Leave notes with timestamps and I'll apply them all in one pass. Extra rounds are available if you need them.

How long does a video take?

A reel usually takes 1–2 days and a YouTube video 3–4 days. Rush delivery is possible depending on my schedule.

Can you match my existing style or brand?

Yes. Send a few videos you like or your brand guide, and I'll build a template for fonts, captions, colors and transitions so every video matches.

Do you provide music?

I use licensed tracks from stock libraries, or music you supply. Every track is cleared for the platform it's posted on.

How do payments work?

50% to start and 50% before final delivery. Payoneer, bank transfer, or bKash for clients in Bangladesh.
