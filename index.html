How I work

A clear process from footage to final export.

Every project starts with understanding the story, then moves step by step to a finished video.

STEP 1

Brief & Discovery

We talk about your goal, audience, references you like and where the video will be posted.

STEP 2

Footage & Plan

You share your files by Google Drive or WeTransfer. I review everything and plan the structure of the edit.

STEP 3

First Cut

A complete edit with music, graphics and a first grade, shared as a private review link.

STEP 4

Feedback & Refinement

You leave timestamped notes and I refine the edit until it's clear, polished and ready.

STEP 5

Final Delivery

Final exports in every size and format you need, plus project files on request.

Client Words

Answers to Common Questions.

How do I send my footage?+

Upload it to Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer and share the link with me. For big shoots I can send you a folder to upload into.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on the length and how much motion graphics or VFX the project needs. You'll get a clear delivery date with the quote.

Are revisions included?+

Yes. We agree on the number of revision rounds before we start, and you can leave timestamped notes on each version.

Can you match my existing style or brand?+

Yes. Send a few videos you like or your brand guide, and I'll keep fonts, titles, color and transitions consistent across your videos.

Do you also do VFX and motion graphics?+

Yes. Along with editing I handle motion graphics, VFX compositing like object and car replacement, and color grading, so one person covers the whole pipeline.

How do we get started?+

Send me your project details through the form below, by email or on WhatsApp. I'll reply with a plan, a price and a delivery date.
